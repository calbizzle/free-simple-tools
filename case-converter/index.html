Case converter

How it works

Title Case capitalises each word but keeps short joining words (a, an, the, and, of, in, on, to and similar) lowercase unless they start the text. Sentence case lowercases everything, then capitalises the first letter of each sentence. camelCase, snake_case and kebab-case strip punctuation and join words, which is handy for file names and code.

Is my text private?

Yes. Conversion happens in your browser; nothing is sent to a server.
