Free Simple Tools

Free, private browser tools

Small tools that run entirely in your browser. Nothing you type or upload is sent anywhere.

  • Word & character counter
    Words, characters, sentences, reading time.
  • Case converter
    UPPER, lower, Title, Sentence, camelCase, snake_case.
  • Age calculator
    Exact age in years, months and days, plus next birthday.
  • Unit converter
    Length, weight, temperature and volume (metric & imperial).
  • Image compressor
    Shrink JPEG, PNG or WebP; runs in your browser.
  • Australian public holidays
    2026–2027 holidays by state, plus a date checker.
  • Australian business days calculator
    Count or add working days, skipping weekends and state holidays.
  • QR code generator
    Links, text, Wi-Fi or phone; download PNG or SVG. No expiry.
Tools run locally in your browser. No sign-up, no tracking cookies.
