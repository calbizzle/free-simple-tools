How it works

Age is counted in whole years, then whole months, then the remaining days, the same way you'd count on a calendar. If you were born on 29 February, this tool treats your birthday as 28 February in non-leap years. Total days counts calendar days between the two dates, not including the end date.

Is my data private?

Yes. Everything is calculated in your browser; dates are never sent anywhere.
