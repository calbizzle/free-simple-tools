How it works

Saturdays, Sundays and full-day public holidays for the selected state or territory are not counted. Part-day holidays (such as Christmas Eve evenings in some states) are treated as normal business days. The end date is always included; the start date is included only if you tick the box. Holiday data covers 2026 and 2027 and comes from the same official sources as our Australian public holidays tool.

This is a guide, not legal advice. Contracts, courts and government agencies can define "business day" differently, and local holidays vary. Check the rules that apply to you.

Is my data private?

Yes. Calculations run entirely in your browser; nothing is sent anywhere.
