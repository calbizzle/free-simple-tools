How it works

Words are counted as runs of letters or numbers separated by spaces or punctuation. Reading time assumes about 230 words per minute. Many forms, job applications and grant portals cap answers by characters including spaces, so check against the "Characters" figure unless the form says otherwise.

Is my text private?

Yes. The counting happens in your browser with JavaScript; your text is never sent to a server.
